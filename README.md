# AI Dropshipping

An operating repo for building a single-product dropshipping business, using AI to
compress the expensive parts: product research, creative production, and analysis.

This is not a code project (yet). It is the written operating system for the business —
the research, the numbers, and the decision gates that keep us from spending money on a
product that was never going to work.

## What's here

## Operating principles

1. **Fulfillment is solved before a dollar goes to ads.** Ads on top of a broken supply
   chain just buys chargebacks faster.
2. **Every product clears the math before it gets a store.** Break-even ROAS is derived
   from actual landed cost, not chosen because it sounds achievable.
3. **Gates are real.** Each phase ends in a go/no-go with a written criterion. If we miss
   it, we cut and re-test rather than lowering the bar.
4. **Claims get substantiated before they run.** The fastest way to lose the business is
   an ad account ban or an FTC problem, not a bad CPA.
